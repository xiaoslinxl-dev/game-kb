# Genshin Impact — Social Features & LiveOps

## LiveOps Framework: The 6-Week Release Cadence

miHoYo maintains one of the industry's most disciplined, rigid LiveOps release pipelines:

- **6-Week Patch Cycle:** Every 6 weeks, a major numbered version update drops (e.g., 4.8, 5.0, 5.1).
- **Annual Region Expansion:** Every X.0 update introduces a massive new geographical continent / nation (e.g., 2.0 Inazuma, 3.0 Sumeru, 4.0 Fontaine, 5.0 Natlan) featuring distinct regional mechanics, music, Archon Quests, and environmental themes.
- **Livestream Previews:** 2 weeks prior to each patch, a developer livestream reveals new characters, events, promotional Primogem codes, and quality-of-life updates.

## Flagship & Mini Events

Each 6-week version contains:
- **1 Major Flagship Event:** Fully voiced event story, mini-games, and a free 4-star character or weapon reward (e.g., Lantern Rite, Windblume Festival, Summer Archipelago).
- **3–4 Minor Events:** Tactical combat trials, puzzle events, or alchemy/cooking mini-games awarding Primogems and upgrade materials.

## Social Features & Co-Op Dynamics

Genshin Impact is primarily designed as a single-player action RPG experience with optional drop-in multiplayer:

- **Co-Op Mode (Unlocked at AR 16):** Up to 4 players can join a host player's world to clear domains, defeat open-world bosses, or explore together.
- **Restrictions in Co-Op:** Main Archon quests and certain story domains are disabled. World interaction is gated to host permissions.
- **Genius Invokation TCG:** Permanent in-game collectible card game featuring casual and direct player-vs-player (PvP) match capabilities.
- **Serenitea Pot:** Housing and customization sub-game allowing players to build custom islands, invite friends, and construct custom structures.
