# Genshin Impact — Overview

## Product Profile

- **Developer / Publisher:** miHoYo / HoYoverse (Cognosphere)
- **Release Date:** September 28, 2020
- **Platforms:** PC, iOS, Android, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Cloud Platforms
- **Engine:** Unity
- **Genre:** Open-World Action Role-Playing Game (ARPG) / Gacha
- **Business Model:** Free-to-Play (F2P) with microtransactions (Gacha Wish system, subscription cards, battle pass)

## Executive Summary

Genshin Impact is a landmark cross-platform open-world action RPG set in the fantasy world of Teyvat. Players assume the role of the "Traveler", searching for their lost twin while journeying across seven distinct nations, each corresponding to an elemental affinity and inspired by real-world cultures (e.g., Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, Snezhnaya).

The title revolutionized free-to-play mobile and console gaming by combining AAA open-world exploration (akin to *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild*) with character-driven anime aesthetics, complex elemental real-time combat, and a high-yield gacha monetization model.

## Key Mechanics & Structure

1. **Elemental Synergy Combat:** Real-time action combat driven by elemental status applications (Pyro, Hydro, Anemo, Electro, Dendro, Cryo, Geo) and multi-character switching.
2. **Exploration & World Design:** Expansive seamless terrain featuring environmental puzzles, climbing, gliding, swimming, and regional traversal mechanics.
3. **Character Roster:** A growing pool of over 80 playable 4-star and 5-star characters, unlocked through story progression or gacha banners.
4. **Cross-Platform Sync:** Unified account architecture allowing cross-save and cross-play across mobile, PC, and consoles.
